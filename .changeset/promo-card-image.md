**PromoCard:** New optional `image` prop, taking the same `Picture` props as the `ScoreCard`. The image bleeds to the edges of the card above the content at 16:9, and sits beside it in `wide` and `fluid` cards from `md`. Cards with an image get the `ilo--card__hasimage` class.
